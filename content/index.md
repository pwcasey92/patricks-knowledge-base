#  The Archmage's Compendium of Forbidden Disciplines

Welcome! This site is all about multiclassing in Dungeons & Dragons. I'll be looking at different class combinations, min-maxing, and ways to make some seriously powerful characters. Whether it's combining classes that work really well together or finding weird builds that somehow turn out amazing, I want to see just how far you can push a character.
